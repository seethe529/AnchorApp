## Description

Anchor is an educational mental health support app designed specifically for veterans and individuals living with PTSD and trauma-related conditions. Built with evidence-based DBT and CBT techniques from authoritative sources including Harvard Medical School, Mayo Clinic, the American Psychological Association, and the VA National Center for PTSD, Anchor provides immediate access to coping strategies, crisis resources, and AI-powered support whenever you need it.

### Key Features

**Evidence-Based DBT/CBT Techniques (with Medical Citations)**
- Grounding exercises (5-4-3-2-1, Body Scan, Physical Grounding, etc.)
- Breathing techniques (Box Breathing, 4-7-8, Diaphragmatic, etc.)
- Distress tolerance skills (TIPP, ACCEPTS, Self-Soothe, etc.)
- Emotion regulation strategies (PLEASE, Opposite Action, Check the Facts, etc.)
- Interpersonal effectiveness (DEAR MAN, GIVE, etc.)
- Mindfulness practices (Observe, Describe, Participate, etc.)
- Cognitive techniques (Thought Records, Behavioral Activation, etc.)
- All techniques include citations to reputable medical sources

**AI Support Assistant**
- Real-time technique suggestions based on your emotional state
- Crisis moment guidance with intelligent keyword detection
- Trauma-informed, validating responses
- Quick help buttons for immediate support
- Powered by advanced natural language understanding

**Enhanced Mood & Emotion Tracking**
- Quick 5-point mood scale for fast check-ins
- Detailed emotion logging with PTSD-appropriate emotion model
- Track primary emotions, intensity, and context
- Identify patterns in hypervigilance, shutdown responses, and emotional states
- Progress charts and trend visualization
- Data export for healthcare providers

**24/7 Crisis Resources**
- National Suicide Prevention Lifeline (988)
- Veterans Crisis Line
- Crisis Text Line
- Local emergency resources
- Personal safety planning tools

**Progress Tracking**
- Daily mood logging with progress charts
- Technique effectiveness ratings
- Identify patterns and triggers
- Data export for healthcare providers

**Privacy & Security**
- All data stored locally on your device
- No cloud storage without your consent
- No tracking or analytics
- HIPAA-aware design
- Secure storage for sensitive information

**100% Offline Capable**
- All features work without internet
- Access techniques anytime, anywhere
- Crisis resources available offline

### Designed For

### Important Disclaimer
Anchor is a self-help and educational tool. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical services. If you're experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact emergency services or a crisis hotline immediately.

Created with input from mental health professionals and trauma survivors, Anchor combines evidence-based techniques with modern technology to provide educational support when you need it most. Whether you're in therapy or managing symptoms on your own, Anchor is your companion for learning coping skills and personal growth.

- Customize your mood check-in time to fit your schedule

**Enhanced Technique Guidance** — We've improved several DBT/CBT techniques based on feedback:
- 5-4-3-2-1 Technique - Now includes guidance to touch items and notice textures (especially cold items), plus date/time grounding to help orient yourself in the present moment
- Cold Grounding - Added sternum placement technique to help stimulate the vagus nerve for better nervous system regulation
- Name 3 Things - Includes date/time orientation to help ground yourself when feeling disconnected
- TIPP - Added important note that intense exercise or extreme temperatures can trigger some people. If this happens to you, focus on paced breathing and muscle relaxation instead
- ACCEPTS (Push Away) - Enhanced with two-layer lock containment visualization for better distress tolerance
- Pros and Cons - Added 24-hour waiting rule: wait a full day after experiencing intense emotions before making important decisions
- Safe Place Visualization - Now explicitly lists all five senses (sight, sound, touch, smell, taste) with examples to make the visualization more vivid and effective

**Progress Screen Improvement** — Added a supportive reminder that progress with PTSD is not always linear. Some days are great, some days are difficult, but fluctuating moods don't mean you're not making progress. Be patient with yourself.

All improvements are based on professional clinical recommendations for trauma-informed care.
