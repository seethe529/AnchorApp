## Description

Anchor is a comprehensive mental health support app designed specifically for veterans and individuals with PTSD. Built on evidence-based DBT and CBT techniques, Anchor provides immediate access to proven coping strategies, crisis resources, and AI-powered support—all in a trauma-informed, easy-to-use interface.

**28 Evidence-Based DBT/CBT Techniques (with Medical Citations)**

**Grounding Techniques:**
- 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding
- Progressive Muscle Relaxation
- Safe Place Visualization
- Cold Grounding
- Name 3 Things

**Breathing Exercises:**
- Box Breathing
- 4-7-8 Breathing
- Resonant Breathing
- Physiological Sigh
- Triangle Breathing

**Distress Tolerance Skills:**
- TIPP (Temperature, Intense Exercise, Paced Breathing, Paired Muscle Relaxation)
- ACCEPTS (Activities, Contributing, Comparisons, Emotions, Pushing Away, Thoughts, Sensations)
- Self-Soothe with Five Senses
- Radical Acceptance

**Emotion Regulation:**
- PLEASE (Physical Health, Eating, Avoiding Substances, Sleep, Exercise)
- Opposite Action
- Check the Facts
- Pros and Cons
- Self-Validation

**Interpersonal Effectiveness:**
- DEAR MAN (Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce, Mindful, Appear Confident, Negotiate)
- GIVE (Gentle, Interested, Validate, Easy Manner)

**Mindfulness:**
- Observe
- Describe
- Participate
- Half-Smile and Willing Hands

**Cognitive Techniques:**
- Thought Records
- Behavioral Activation
- Exposure Therapy Principles
